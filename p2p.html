3 · Direct peer-to-peer

WebRTC with no signalling server. The two devices swap their connection details through QR codes, then send text and files straight to each other, encrypted.

On the Mac

  1. Press “Show connection code”, then scan it with the phone’s camera app. It opens this page on the phone.
  2. The phone shows a reply code. Press “Scan reply” here and hold the phone up to the Mac’s webcam.

With that box ticked, each device asks Google’s public STUN server for its own internet address. Nothing you send goes through it. Untick it to stay on the local Wi-Fi only.
